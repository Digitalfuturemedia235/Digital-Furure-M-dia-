Who we are

About Digital Future Media

Founded in 2009 as Diaspora Future Media, Digital Future Media (DFM) is an independent media and communications initiative created by Gore Mbaïtoloum.

Over the years, DFM has documented diplomatic activities, official visits, international events, people and development initiatives through photography, video and documentary work.

From Washington, DC, to international institutions and Africa, DFM preserves a visual record of events, encounters and stories that deserve to be remembered.
